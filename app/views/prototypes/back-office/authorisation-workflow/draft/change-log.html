{% extends "prototypes/back-office/layouts/authorisation-workflow.html" %} {# Notes on design mock ==================== Authorisation workflow: change log (draft v2) What it is for -------------- A record of what has changed in the service and who needs to do what, across claims and agreements. Some changes are just saved. Some wait for a team lead to approve them, and are then approved or rejected. Design ------ 1. One row per change. A change can cover several details saved at once, as one entry, matching the claim history. When a team lead approves or rejects a change, the same row's status changes and shows who decided and when. Approvals are not rows of their own. 2. Opens on all changes, newest first. If any changes are waiting for approval, inset text says how many and links to just those. 3. Columns: - Date - Type: Claim or Agreement, in bold so they are easy to pick out - Number: the claim or agreement number, linking to it. Claims have the claim type below, as on the claims list. The scheme is not shown, as agreement numbers already start with it (IAHW, AHWR or PBR), and the Scheme filter covers it. The flags list uses the same Type and Number columns. - Change: a short summary, with the details listed below when there is more than one. The from and to values are in the record's history, to keep rows short. - Made by: name, linking to the user, with their role at the time of the change (the internalBy macro). Assisted Digital shows as text. - Status: Pending, Approved or Rejected, with "Waiting for a team lead" or who decided and when below. Changes that did not need approval say "No approval needed", with no tag. No Action column. The number is the link. Pending rows have a "Review" link under the status, to draft/review-change, where a team lead approves or rejects the change. Decided rows have "View decision". After a decision, a success banner shows here once. 4. Filters, in a row of dropdowns: - Type: claims or agreements - Type of change: the types belong to claims or agreements. With all records, both lists show, with "Claim:" and "Agreement:" in front. Once a record is chosen and the filters applied, only that record's types show. GOV.UK dropdowns cannot group options, and changing one dropdown from another would need bespoke JavaScript. - Scheme: IAHW or PBR - Status: waiting for approval, approved, rejected or no approval needed - Made by: each internal user, or Assisted Digital The claim type filter (review or follow-up) is gone. Claim type shows under the number. Built only with GOV.UK Frontend macros and classes. No bespoke CSS, HTML components or JS. The Date column shows the time below the date, and the Status column the time of a team lead's decision, as the history records the time of each event. Rows are newest first by date and time. Requests to withdraw a claim (draft/withdraw-claim) show here as "Claim withdrawal requested", waiting for a team lead, like changes that need approval. The "When" filter shows changes made in the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. The team lead's daily email (draft/emails/team-lead-daily) links to the change log filtered to changes waiting for approval, and to those made in the last 24 hours. Sorting ------- The table uses the MOJ Design System sortable table (MOJ Frontend is already installed and loaded by the kit): select a column heading to sort by it. Only some columns sort, where the order helps: date, type, made by and status (waiting for approval first). GOV.UK Frontend has no sortable table, but the MOJ one is an established cross-government pattern for case-working tools, built on GOV.UK Frontend, with accessible sort buttons. It sorts the rows on the page only. A live service with pagination would sort on the server, across every result. Sorting uses data-sort-value where the cell text would sort wrongly, for example dates. Title, type, version and last updated date for this design are in app/views/prototypes/back-office/data/designs.js. Update them there when the design changes. Data ---- changeLogEntries and changeLogTypes come from app/routes.js: changes made in the prototype through the change claim details flow (on claim FUBC-HJ9N-S5PK), then the illustrative entries in app/views/prototypes/back-office/data/change-log.js. How the filters work -------------------- A GET form. The Prototype Kit saves query string values in session data, so the template reads data.changeLogRecord, changeLogType, changeLogScheme, changeLogStatus and changeLogBy. They are not shared with the current change log. Prototype limitations --------------------- - Every claim links to the one draft claim details page, and every agreement to the one draft agreement details page. - No pagination, as the data is short. To confirm with the BA ---------------------- - The full list of agreement changes (flags, Assisted Digital, anything else) and which need approval. - Whether team leads can approve their own changes.
