{% extends "prototypes/back-office/layouts/authorisation-workflow.html" %} {# Notes on design mock ==================== Authorisation workflow: change log (draft v2) What it is for -------------- A record of what has changed in the service and who needs to do what, across claims and agreements. Some changes are just saved. Some wait for a team lead to approve them, and are then approved or rejected. Design ------ 1. One row per change. A change can cover several details saved at once, as one entry, matching the claim history. When a team lead approves or rejects a change, the same row's status changes and shows who decided and when. Approvals are not rows of their own. 2. Opens on all changes, newest first. If any changes are waiting for approval, inset text says how many and links to just those. 3. Columns: - Date - Record: the claim or agreement number, linking to it, with what it is below: "Claim · IAHW · Follow-up" or "Agreement · PBR" - Change: a short summary, with the details listed below when there is more than one. The from and to values are in the record's history, to keep rows short. - Made by: name, linking to the user, with their role at the time of the change (the internalBy macro). Assisted Digital shows as text. - Status: Pending, Approved or Rejected, with "Waiting for a team lead" or who decided and when below. Changes that did not need approval say "No approval needed", with no tag. No Action column. The record number is the link. Pending rows have a "Review" link under the status, to draft/review-change, where a team lead approves or rejects the change. Decided rows have "View decision". After a decision, a success banner shows here once. 4. Filters, in a row of dropdowns: - Record: claims or agreements - Type of change: the types belong to claims or agreements. With all records, both lists show, with "Claim:" and "Agreement:" in front. Once a record is chosen and the filters applied, only that record's types show. GOV.UK dropdowns cannot group options, and changing one dropdown from another would need bespoke JavaScript. - Scheme: IAHW or PBR - Status: waiting for approval, approved, rejected or no approval needed - Made by: each internal user, or Assisted Digital The claim type filter (review or follow-up) is gone. Claim type shows in the Record column. Built only with GOV.UK Frontend macros and classes. No bespoke CSS, HTML components or JS. The Date column shows the time below the date, and the Status column the time of a team lead's decision, as the history records the time of each event. Rows are newest first by date and time. Requests to withdraw a claim (draft/withdraw-claim) show here as "Claim withdrawal requested", waiting for a team lead, like changes that need approval. Title, type, version and last updated date for this design are in app/views/prototypes/back-office/data/designs.js. Update them there when the design changes.
