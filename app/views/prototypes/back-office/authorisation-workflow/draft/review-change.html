{% extends "prototypes/back-office/layouts/authorisation-workflow.html" %} {# Notes on design mock ==================== Authorisation workflow: review a change (draft v1) Where a team lead approves or rejects a change waiting for approval. Opened from "Review" on a pending row in the change log. Each change has its own page: draft/review-change/. The page shows: - what the change is to, who made it and their role at the time, when and why - each detail changed, from and to - "Do you approve this change?": Approve or Reject. Approve reveals optional notes. Reject reveals a required "Why are you rejecting this change?". Both are recorded in the history, so the person who made the change can see them. "Save decision" goes back to the change log with a success banner. An approved change updates the claim or agreement. A rejected one does not. The change log row and the claim history show the decision, who made it and when, and the claim history shows the notes. Once decided, the same page shows the decision instead of the form. "View decision" in the change log opens it. A team lead cannot review their own change. The page says so instead of showing the form. Claim withdrawal requests (from draft/withdraw-claim) are reviewed here too. Instead of a from and to table, the page shows the answers from the withdraw claim form, and the Approve hint warns that a withdrawal cannot be undone and the farmer will no longer see the claim. Approving withdraws the claim. Rejecting leaves it in check. Dates show the time too, as the history records the time of each event. The signed-in user is Mei Chen, the team lead persona, as only team leads can approve. In the live service only team leads would see the Review link. Errors use the GOV.UK error summary and error messages: no decision chosen, or rejected without notes. Built only with GOV.UK Frontend macros and classes. The notes boxes use the radios component's conditional reveal, which is GOV.UK Frontend JavaScript, not bespoke code. Title, type, version and last updated date for this design are in app/views/prototypes/back-office/data/designs.js. Update them there when the design changes. The page is rendered by app/routes.js, with the change as `change`, the reviewer's persona key as `reviewer`, and `errors` and `values` when the form has errors.
