Back office 2.0 optimisation: design note

Who can change what

Every detail on a claim and an agreement: whether it can be changed in the back office, and who can change it.

Steve Molesworth, with Olayinka (BA)
First pass, 20 November 2026

{{ govukWarningText({ text: "For the team to discuss with the RPA. Not part of the service." }) }}

There are 2 types of back office user: case officers and team leads. With the authorisation workflow, a case officer can change some details straight away, and request a change to others for a team lead to approve or reject. Which needs approval should depend on how much a change could affect the payment.

  • Can change: they can change it, with no approval
  • Can request: they can ask, and a team lead approves or rejects it. No one can approve their own request
  • No: it cannot be changed in the back office
  • To agree: our suggestion, to agree with the RPA

What each type of user can do overall is on the personas page.

  1. Do you agree with which details can be changed, and which need approval?
  2. Can any details change after a claim is paid?
  3. Today only super admins can change claim data. Is it right that case officers can change low-risk details, such as the vet’s name, without approval?
  4. Should anyone be able to change details on the agreement, or should they always come from the customer’s record?
